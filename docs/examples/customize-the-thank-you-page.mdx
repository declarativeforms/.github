---
title: "Customize the thank-you page"
---

If you have not created a form before, start with [Create a form](/getting-started/create-a-form).

```yaml form.yaml
version: 1
title: "Example form"
description: "A simple form with a customized thank-you page."

sections:
  - id: section_1
    title: "Section 1"
    fields:
      - id: question_1
        type: short_text
        label: "Question 1"
        validators:
          - required

      - id: question_2
        type: email
        label: "Question 2"
        validators:
          - required

    next: done

completion:
  title: "Thanks for your submission"
  message: "Thanks, {{data.question_1}}. We will reply to {{data.question_2}}."
  button:
    label: "Go to example.com"
    url: "https://example.com"
```
