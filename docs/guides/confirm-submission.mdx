---
title: Send an Applicant Confirmation
description: Email applicants automatically when their submission is received.
---

After notifying the landlord, you can also send a confirmation email to the applicant themselves — using their submitted email address as the recipient.

---

## What to add

Add a second email connection to the `connections` block in `rental.yaml`:

```yaml
connections:
  - type: email
    to: "{{data.email}}"
    subject: "Thanks for applying, {{data.full_name}}"
    body: "<p>We received your application and will be in touch within 2 business days.</p>"
```

---

## How it works

Setting `to: "{{data.email}}"` uses the applicant's submitted email address as the recipient. The `{{data.email}}` template resolves to the value the applicant entered in the `email` field.

`subject` and `body` also support template syntax. In this example, `{{data.full_name}}` personalizes the subject line with the applicant's name.

`body` accepts HTML. Keep it simple — plain paragraphs and links render reliably across email clients.

For full details on the `{{data.fieldId}}` template syntax, see [Personalize Emails with Templates](/guides/email-templates).
