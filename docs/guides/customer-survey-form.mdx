---
title: Build a Customer Survey Form with Ratings
description: Create a customer satisfaction survey with a 1–5 rating scale and endpoint labels.
---

You'll build a customer satisfaction survey that collects a name, email, a 1–5 rating, and an optional comment. By the end you'll understand how `type: rating` renders an interactive scale and how `min_label` and `max_label` add descriptive labels to the endpoints.

---

## Define the form

Create a file called `customer_survey.yaml` in your repository:

```yaml
version: 1
title: Customer Survey
description: Help us improve — rate your experience and share your thoughts.

sections:
  - id: survey
    title: Your Feedback
    fields:
      - id: name
        type: short_text
        label: Name
        placeholder: Jane Smith
        validators:
          - required

      - id: email
        type: email
        label: Email
        placeholder: jane@example.com
        validators:
          - required

      - id: satisfaction
        type: rating
        label: How satisfied are you with our service?
        min_label: Very dissatisfied
        max_label: Very satisfied
        validators:
          - required

      - id: comments
        type: long_text
        label: Any additional comments?
        placeholder: Tell us more about your experience.
    next: done
```

---

## Deploy it

Commit and push the file to your repository:

```bash
git add customer_survey.yaml
git commit -m "add customer survey form"
git push
```

Then open your form at:

```
https://app.declarativeforms.com/<organization>/<repository>/customer_survey
```

(The `.yaml` extension is not included in the URL.)
