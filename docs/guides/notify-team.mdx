---
title: Notify Your Team by Email
description: Send an email to your team when someone submits the form.
---

Once a submission completes, you'll want to know about it. This guide adds a landlord notification email to `rental.yaml`.

---

## What to add

Add a `connections` block at the root level of `rental.yaml`:

```yaml
connections:
  - type: email
    to: landlord@example.com
    subject: "New application from {{data.full_name}}"
    include_responses: true
```

---

## Connection properties

| Property | Type | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| `type` | string | `email` for email connections |
| `to` | string | Recipient address. |
| `subject` | string | Email subject line. Supports `{{data.fieldId}}` templates — see [Personalize Emails with Templates](/guides/email-templates). |
| `include_responses` | boolean | When `true`, appends a formatted summary of all submitted fields to the email body. |

---

## When connections fire

Connections fire once when the submission `status` becomes `completed` — that is, when the final section is submitted and `next` resolves to `done`. They do not fire for partial saves (mid-flow section submissions).
