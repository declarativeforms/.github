---
title: Set Open and Close Dates
description: Control when your form accepts submissions.
---

Adding `start_date` and `end_date` to `rental.yaml` makes the form unavailable before or after those dates — useful for application periods with a fixed deadline.

---

## What to add

Add `start_date` and `end_date` at the root level of `rental.yaml`:

```yaml
start_date: "2024-01-01T00:00:00Z"
end_date: "2026-12-31T23:59:59Z"
```

---

## How it works

Both values are ISO 8601 datetime strings in UTC. The platform checks the current time against these bounds when the form URL is requested:

- Before `start_date` — the form displays a "not yet open" message.
- After `end_date` — the form displays a "closed" message.
- Within the window — the form loads normally.

Both keys are optional. Omit `start_date` to make the form available immediately. Omit `end_date` to keep it open indefinitely.

In-progress submissions that were started before the end date can still be completed after it closes — the date check only applies to the initial form load.
