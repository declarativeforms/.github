---
title: Completion
description: How to customize the screen shown after a form is submitted.
---

When a form is submitted, the platform displays a default thank-you page. The `completion` property on the form lets you customize it with your own heading, message, and call-to-action.

```yaml
version: 1
title: Contact Form

sections:
  - id: contact
    title: Get in Touch
    fields:
      - id: name
        type: short_text
        label: Name
      - id: email
        type: email
        label: Email
    next: done

completion:
  title: "Thanks, {{data.name}}!"
  message: We'll be in touch within 24 hours.
  button:
    label: Visit our website
    url: https://example.com
```

- **`title`** — The heading on the completion page.
- **`message`** — Body text below the title.
- **`button`** — An optional call-to-action with a `label` and `url`.

---

## Templating

All three properties support Handlebars templating. Use `{{data.fieldId}}` to insert a submitted value into the completion screen.

```yaml
completion:
  title: "Welcome, {{data.name}}!"
  message: "A confirmation has been sent to {{data.email}}."
  button:
    label: View your profile
    url: "https://example.com/users/{{data.email}}"
```

- **`{{data.fieldId}}`** — Replaced with the submitted value for the field matching that `id`. Works in `title`, `message`, `button.label`, and `button.url`.
